Sound

Short clips from the recordings, as they were and with the sound enhanced. Press play, then switch versions: the clip carries on from the same moment. The focus is always the spoken word.

  1. Indoors: hum and rumble removed, every recording brought to the same loudness (one setting for the whole recording, so pauses stay quiet), and loud and soft speech brought a little closer.
  2. Outdoors: the voice brought forward over crowd and wind (the low rumble and the hiss above speech cut, the range where words are clearest lifted), without lifting the background.

The recordings themselves haven’t been changed yet.
