Cleaned sound: before and after

Forty seconds from six recordings, each with a different problem. Press play, then switch between Original and Cleaned: the clip carries on from the same moment. The recordings themselves haven’t been changed.

  1. Hum and rumble removed: everything below the lowest voices, and the 50 Hz mains drone.
  2. Every recording brought to the same loudness, with one volume setting for the whole recording, so pauses stay quiet.
  3. Loud and soft speech brought a little closer, so a distant speaker is easier to follow.
